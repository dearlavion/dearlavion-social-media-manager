# TravelBesty — Brand Voice & Content Guide

Source: Fundamentals of Marketing coursework (customer persona, positioning, funnel plan, promotions plan). This doc distills that work into a reusable reference for writing captions, replies, and any future AI-assisted content for TravelBesty's social channels.

## What TravelBesty is

A free **Travel Kit Builder** that generates a personalized packing list based on destination, trip length, weather, and planned activities — paired with a **one-stop shop** to buy the recommended essentials in one place, one shipping fee.

**Positioning:** Offers a free Travel Kit Builder. One-stop shop for all travel essentials. Highly curated and tested travel kits.

## Tagline & core lines

Reuse these verbatim where they fit — they're the tested language, not just inspiration:

- **Tagline:** Personalized Travel Essentials
- **Headline (pain-first):** Stop forgetting travel essentials.
- **Subheadline:** Get a free personalized travel kit and shop everything you need in one place.
- **Promise line:** Free. Personalized. Nothing extra.
- **Warmth line:** The best part of traveling shouldn't be getting ready to travel. So we handle that part — like a good friend would.
- **Primary CTA:** Build My Free Travel Kit

## Who we're talking to

**Mia, 23, Pasig** — working professional, travels 3-5x/year (domestic + international), disposable income, mobile-first and social-native (lives on TikTok, Instagram, Facebook).

- **Wants:** to be well-prepared without overpacking; to save time not shopping across a dozen sites; recommendations tailored to her actual trip.
- **Pain points:** forgets essentials; buys unnecessary items while missing important ones; multiple shipping fees and arrival-anxiety from scattered/overseas orders; cheap items that break in small packing spaces.
- **Trusts:** organic reviews and demo videos that show the *exact* item in real use (not polished ads) on TikTok; a well-designed, consistent profile as a credibility signal on Instagram.
- **Buys on:** lightweight/packable design, reasonable price (but will pay more once quality/trust is established), reusable items, unique design.

## Voice principles

1. **Name the fear, then hand her the fix.** Lead with the pain point in plain language ("Stop forgetting travel essentials"), immediately followed by the solution — never bury the offer.
2. **Friend, not salesperson.** Warm and reassuring, like someone who's already packed for this trip and is looking out for you — not a brand pitching at you.
3. **Specific over polished.** Real trip examples, real packing lists, real reviews beat generic lifestyle imagery. (This is *why* her own laptop-sleeve purchase decision worked — seeing her exact laptop model fit the sleeve.) Show the exact kit for the exact trip type where possible.
4. **No overselling.** "Free. Personalized. Nothing extra." — the brand's whole promise is the absence of bloat. Copy should mirror that: short, no filler adjectives, no fake urgency.
5. **Prepared-traveler, not luxury-influencer.** She's practical and a little anxious about forgetting things, not chasing aspirational travel-influencer aesthetics. Keep it grounded.

## Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Stop forgetting your charger. Here's what's actually in a 9-day BKK carry-on kit." | "Elevate your travel game with our exclusive collection ✈️" |
| Show a real, specific trip (destination, duration, activity) | Generic "wanderlust" stock-photo captions |
| One clear CTA: build the free kit | Multiple competing CTAs in one post |
| Short, plain sentences | Overwrought travel-poetry copy |

## Content pillars by funnel stage

Use this to decide what a given post/caption should actually be doing:

- **Awareness** (reach new people scrolling for travel content): "Stop forgetting...", "Things I brought to [destination]...", "The smallest carry-on towel...", packing mistakes, destination guides, what-to-pack videos.
- **Consideration** (convince a follower to visit the site): demo the free Travel Kit Builder, customer testimonials, personalized kit examples, "one shop, one shipping fee" messaging.
- **Conversion** (get the click): frictionless framing — it's free to build the kit — clear CTA, highlight real reviews.
- **Loyalty** (repeat + referral): encourage sharing the finished kit, reviews, trip photos; seasonal/new-product nudges after a trip.

## Platform notes

- **TikTok:** organic, human, real-person demo energy — this is where authenticity outperforms polish.
- **Instagram:** aesthetic and consistency matter more here — it's the credibility check after TikTok gets attention.
- **Facebook:** secondary channel, still worth ad reach per the coursework's market data.

## Campaign targets (context, not copy)

100 new followers/month/channel; roughly 1 kit sold per 100 followers — useful context for prioritizing which funnel stage needs the most content in a given push.
